# Harbour guide (EXAMPLE document, replace with your own)

The harbour at Kelmouth opens its main gate at six in the morning and closes it at nine in the evening. Fishing boats unload their catch at the east quay, where the fish market starts selling at half past six. Visitors who arrive by car park in the field behind the lifeboat station, which costs four pounds for the whole day.

The lighthouse on the north breakwater was built in 1874 and is forty metres tall. Tours run every hour from ten until four, and each tour takes forty minutes. Children under eight are not allowed on the top gallery because the spiral stairs are narrow and steep. Tickets are sold only at the keeper's cottage and cannot be booked in advance.

The ferry to Saint Brannock leaves from the west quay twice a day, at eleven and at three. The crossing takes twenty five minutes in calm weather, and the company cancels sailings when the wind is above force six. Dogs travel free if they stay on a lead, but bicycles must be booked a day ahead because the ferry carries only six.

The harbour café serves breakfast until eleven and hot lunches until two. It is closed on Mondays throughout the winter, from the first of November to the end of March. The public toilets by the fish market are open whenever the gate is open.
